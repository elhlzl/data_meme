# data_meme
a digital humanities project

How can an ethically sourced, small-scale AI model support Meme Studies research without dependence on corporate AI providers? Having a background in media studies with a special interest in memes and social media culture, I want to focus on a way to sort those accumulated collections of memes whilst also asking about the possibilities and limit of integrating AI into Meme Studies research without depending on big companies like Anthropic, OpenAI or DeepSeek. 

The projects’ goal is to have an AI Model, that follows a more ethical approach, extract information from different screenshots of memes and add them to a database whilst also extracting queries from it. The database should include the pop-cultural moment it references, the emotions it might try to transpose, describe what it depicts, the citation data including a source link, a link to the .png file, cite the pages of the academic texts it used, reference if and where the meme uses black culture and AAVE (African American Vernacular English). The idea is to assist researchers in Meme Studies with their findings and research materials. 

To create this database, the project draws on selected texts on Meme Studies, the Meme Blog knowyourmeme.com and on various Memes by Instagram meme-creators collected by me and my friends in our everyday life. Since Meme Studies mostly comes from an academic viewpoint, it might not consider pop cultural voices and references might be unaccounted for. Nevertheless, the impact of AAVE on Memes and pop culture should be acknowledged and worked into the project.

Using an AI system in this project helps to expand the database and to handle the number of memes researches are working with. It should however, also have a chat function, where queries for references of the memes, texts, emotions and events within the database can be made and assist what the human mind might not be aware of. Since emotions regarding memes are an individual matter the results of an AI might be misleading and should be double checked. Instead of seeing the skills of a smaller model as an obstacle, their limits are considered as part of the research process.

Last the ethical and colonial aspects that come with using an AI are a big part for me and my research. Hence the goal not only lies withing creating this database, but to find a model that actually considers these aspects and is capable of extracting the meme, describing it, while adding difference perspectives. Since the project will be working with AAVE, the bias an LLM might present towards it, should not go unrecognized and will be accounted into the decision process of choosing an AI Model.
